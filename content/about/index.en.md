The Laboratory of Materials Physics (LaFiM) was created in 2023, at the Centre of Exact and Technological Sciences of the Federal University of Maranhão (UFMA), in São Luís, under the coordination of Prof. Dr. João Victor Barbosa Moura.

It was created to establish an environment dedicated to the development of experimental research in Materials Physics and to the training of undergraduate and graduate students. Since then, LaFiM has been consolidating its activities mainly in the study of micro- and nanostructured inorganic materials, their physical properties and their response to extreme conditions.

The research carried out by the group covers the synthesis of functional materials, the study of their structural and vibrational properties and the investigation of phase transitions induced by pressure and temperature, using different experimental techniques available at UFMA and at partner institutions. These activities are complemented by experiments performed at large scientific facilities, including national and international synchrotron light sources.

This trajectory has allowed LaFiM to establish a growing network of scientific collaborations in Brazil and abroad, to expand the training of human resources and to contribute to the development of Materials Physics research at UFMA.

Although formally created in 2023, LaFiM brings together and consolidates an earlier trajectory of research on inorganic materials, spectroscopy, phase transitions and extreme conditions, establishing at UFMA a space dedicated to the experimental investigation of materials and to the training of new researchers.
