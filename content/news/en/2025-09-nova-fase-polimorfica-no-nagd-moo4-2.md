---
title: "Ceramics International article describes a new polymorphic phase of NaGd(MoO₄)₂"
excerpt: "A study with LaFiM participation obtains a previously unknown crystalline form of sodium gadolinium molybdate and shows, through in situ measurements, that it transforms irreversibly into another structure at around 813 K."
---

The article "A novel polymorphic phase in NaGd(MoO₄)₂: Synthesis and temperature-induced phase transition" was published in Ceramics International, from Elsevier (volume 51, issue 22, 2025). The last author is Prof. Dr. João Victor Barbosa Moura, coordinator of LaFiM.

Sodium gadolinium molybdate, NaGd(MoO₄)₂, is a compound with potential for optoelectronics and temperature-dependent applications. Using a surfactant-free hydrothermal synthesis, the authors obtained a new crystalline form of the material, a fergusonite-type monoclinic polymorph (space group I2/a), characterized by X-ray diffraction and Raman spectroscopy.

The team then followed the sample in situ during heating, by X-ray diffraction and Raman scattering. At around 813 K (about 540 °C), the monoclinic phase transforms irreversibly into a scheelite-type tetragonal structure (space group I4₁/a). The transition is accompanied by continuous adjustments of the lattice parameters and subtle spectral changes, typical of a second-order phase transition, as confirmed by differential scanning calorimetry (DSC).

Refinement of the high-temperature diffraction patterns also revealed the coexistence of two tetragonal phases with slightly different unit-cell parameters.

The results broaden the understanding of polymorphism and high-temperature behaviour in double molybdates and reinforce the material's potential for designing thermally robust functional devices. The topic falls within the laboratory's research line on materials under extreme conditions.

The work brings together researchers from UFMA, the Federal University of Piauí (UFPI), the Federal University of Ceará (UFC) and Harvard University.

Reference: M. L. A. Dorneles, C. C. Santos, C. Luz-Lima, W. C. Ferreira, P. T. C. Freire, A. S. de Menezes and J. V. B. Moura. Ceramics International 51(22), 35582–35591 (2025). DOI: 10.1016/j.ceramint.2025.05.280
