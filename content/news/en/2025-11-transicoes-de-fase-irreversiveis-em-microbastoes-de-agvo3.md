---
title: "Ceramics International article investigates irreversible phase transitions in α-AgVO₃ microrods"
excerpt: "A study with LaFiM participation follows the heating of silver vanadate microrods in situ and identifies a sequence of three phase transitions that are not undone when the material returns to room temperature."
---

The article "Irreversible phase transitions in α-AgVO₃ Microrods: In situ high-temperature structural analysis" was published in Ceramics International, from Elsevier (volume 51, issue 27, 2025). The last author is Prof. Dr. João Victor Barbosa Moura, coordinator of LaFiM.

The study investigates the thermal response and structural phase transitions of silver vanadate microrods in the α phase (α-AgVO₃). The material was obtained by coprecipitation, with a monoclinic structure and a rod-like morphology with diameters and lengths in the micrometre range, and characterized by scanning electron microscopy, X-ray diffraction, Raman scattering, and UV-Vis and infrared (FTIR) spectroscopy.

The team also carried out theoretical calculations using density functional theory (DFT). The calculated vibrational frequencies agreed well with the experimental measurements at room temperature.

In the in situ experiments, with the sample heated from 298 K to 673 K (25 °C to 400 °C), the authors identified a sequence of transformations. At 413 K, the monoclinic structure becomes triclinic (α → α′). At 513 K, a further transition leads to a different triclinic structure (α′ → β′). On cooling, at 433 K, the material adopts a monoclinic structure different from the original one (β′ → β). This is why the transformations are irreversible: back at room temperature, the material does not recover the α phase.

According to the authors, the results provide useful information for the ceramics field, helping to understand the thermal characteristics of α-AgVO₃ and its application prospects. The topic falls within the laboratory's research line on materials under extreme conditions.

The work brings together researchers from UFMA, the State University of Maranhão (UEMA), the Federal University of Piauí (UFPI) and the University of Brasília (UnB).

Reference: A. W. Miranda, E. Moreira, C. C. Santos, C. Luz-Lima, D. L. Azevedo, A. S. de Menezes and J. V. B. Moura. Ceramics International 51(27), 52374–52385 (2025). DOI: 10.1016/j.ceramint.2025.08.443
