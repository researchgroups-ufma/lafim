---
title: "Article on silver dimolybdate nanorods makes the cover of Pharmaceutics"
excerpt: "A study led by the LaFiM coordinator assesses the anticancer activity and in vivo behaviour of m-Ag₂Mo₂O₇ nanorods and was selected for the cover of the journal's March 2025 issue."
---

The article "Silver Dimolybdate Nanorods: In Vitro Anticancer Activity Against Breast and Prostate Tumors and In Vivo Pharmacological Insights" was published in Pharmaceutics (MDPI) and selected for the cover of the March 2025 issue (volume 17, issue 3). The first author is Prof. Dr. João Victor Barbosa Moura, coordinator of LaFiM.

The study investigates monoclinic silver dimolybdate nanorods (m-Ag₂Mo₂O₇), obtained by hydrothermal synthesis and characterized by X-ray diffraction, scanning electron microscopy, and Raman and infrared (FTIR) spectroscopy. The material was tested against two aggressive tumour cell lines: breast cancer (MDA-MB-231) and prostate cancer (PC-3).

The nanorods reduced the viability of breast cancer cells by 23.5% at a concentration of 50 µg/mL, with no significant effect on prostate cancer cells. In the in vivo assays, with the particles labelled with technetium-99m, the material accumulated mainly in the stomach, liver, spleen and bladder, with a half-life of about 6.8 hours and renal clearance.

The authors conclude that, although the standalone cytotoxic effect is modest, the ability to induce oxidative stress and the favourable pharmacokinetic profile point to the material's potential as an adjuvant agent in cancer therapy, particularly for chemoresistant breast tumours.

The work brings together researchers from the Department of Physics at UFMA, the Nuclear Engineering Institute of the Brazilian Nuclear Energy Commission (IEN/CNEN), Rio de Janeiro State University (UERJ), the Federal University of Piauí (UFPI) and Harvard University.

Reference: J. V. B. Moura, N. C. Gomes-da-Silva, L. M. R. Alencar, W. C. Ferreira, C. da Luz Lima and R. Santos-Oliveira. Pharmaceutics 17(3), 298 (2025). DOI: 10.3390/pharmaceutics17030298
