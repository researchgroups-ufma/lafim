---
title: "Review on metal vanadates published in international journal"
excerpt: "Review article organizes the state of the art on metal vanadates and their structural, optical, and catalytic properties."
---

The group published a review article that organizes the state of the art on
metal vanadates, a broad family of materials that has attracted growing
interest in condensed matter physics. The text brings together scattered
results from the literature and proposes a systematization of the main
structural, optical, and catalytic properties of these compounds.

The review discusses how different metal cations influence the lattice
symmetry and, consequently, the vibrational and electronic behavior of the
material. It also presents the experimental techniques most commonly used to
study these systems, with emphasis on Raman spectroscopy and X-ray
diffraction, in addition to the computational approaches that complement the
analysis.

By gathering and contextualizing a large amount of information, the work is
expected to serve as a reference for students and researchers entering the
field, acting as a starting point for new projects both within and beyond the
laboratory.
